# Development plan

Version 0.1.0a3 adds readiness chains without replacing the earlier rule/bundle/Atomic-plan workflows. The initial three chains are narrow discovery utility signals with common legitimate uses. Reports retain unknown coverage and explicitly separate synthetic predicate testing from delivery.

Future steps require separately inspected event-export adapters, authenticated execution evidence and isolated SIEM rule evaluation. Each must prove field mapping, positive/negative behavior and provenance independently. Production deployment and Atomic execution must not become implicit side effects of readiness.
